## Models by family

### AgenticQwen

#### AgenticQwen-30B-A3B (30B, agentic)

MoE agentic language model trained on Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct with multi-round RL. Activates only 3B parameters per forward pass while matching or exceeding the 8B dense model on agentic benchmarks such as BFCL-V4 and TAU-2.

**Capabilities:** tool_use, multi_step_reasoning, agentic_planning, chat

**Downloads:** [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/alibaba-pai/AgenticQwen-30B-A3B) | [ModelScope](https://modelscope.cn/models/PAI/AgenticQwen-30B-A3B)

**Recommended usage in EasyDistill 2:**

- Pipeline: `agent_distillation`
- Backend: `pai_token`
- Notes: Recommended when you need the strongest agentic capability with lower active-parameter inference cost than dense 8B models.

#### AgenticQwen-8B (8B, agentic)

Small agentic language model trained on Qwen3-8B with multi-round reinforcement learning. Designed for multi-step reasoning, tool orchestration, and long-horizon agentic tasks such as flight booking, account management, and data analysis.

**Capabilities:** tool_use, multi_step_reasoning, agentic_planning, chat

**Downloads:** [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/alibaba-pai/AgenticQwen-8B) | [ModelScope](https://modelscope.cn/models/PAI/AgenticQwen-8B)

### AgenticQwen-Data

Synthetic agentic RL training data generated by the dual data flywheels used to train AgenticQwen. Includes behavior-tree-structured tasks, virtual tool definitions, multi-turn trajectories, and rubric-based quality annotations.

### DistilQwen_100K

100K instruction-following samples covering math, code, knowledge Q&A, instruction following, and creative generation. Useful for preventing catastrophic forgetting when fine-tuning DistilQwen models.

### DistilQwen_1M

1M-sample version of the DistilQwen instruction-following dataset. Provides broader coverage for large-scale fine-tuning.

### OmniThought

2M chain-of-thought reasoning traces generated and validated by DeepSeek-R1 and QwQ-32B. Each trace is annotated with RV and CD scores for curriculum mixing.

### OmniThought-0528

365K CoT reasoning traces generated and validated by DeepSeek-R1-0528. Extension of OmniThought with a newer teacher model.

### OmniThoughtV_Raw_1.8M

1.8M raw multi-modal long chain-of-thought traces distilled from Qwen-VL-max on FineVision seed problems. Traces follow the <thinking>/<answer> format and store images as base64 strings.

### OmniThoughtV_Filter_0.5M

Filtered subset of OmniThoughtV selected via RV/CD and correctness scoring with the EasyDistill MMCoT pipeline. Fine-tuning Qwen3-VL 2B/4B/8B on this subset improves both general visual understanding and reasoning-heavy benchmarks.
